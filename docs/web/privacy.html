Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Overview & Our Commitment

At Inkto (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we take your privacy and the confidentiality of your documents with the utmost seriousness. Inkto is engineered specifically for legal practitioners, professionals, and students who transcribe sensitive handwritten notes, court pleadings, affidavits, and voice dictations.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is processed, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect & Permissions

To provide high-fidelity transcription and document scanning, Inkto requests the following device permissions and collects the following data strictly when used:

  • Camera & Scanned Documents: Camera access is used exclusively via the Google ML Kit Document Scanner to capture and digitize physical handwritten notes and paper documents on your device.
  • Microphone & Audio Recordings: Microphone access is requested only when you initiate dictation or record court proceedings within the app. Audio files are temporarily processed for transcription.
  • Account Information (Email Address): We collect your email address when you sign in via one-time passcode (OTP). This is used solely to authenticate your account, synchronize your subscription plan, and maintain your workspace.
  • Device Hardware Identifier (Pseudonymous): A cryptographic SHA-256 hash of your device hardware identifier is generated locally to manage free-trial quotas and prevent abuse across unauthenticated burner sessions. It cannot be linked back to your real-world identity.

3. How We Process & Protect Your Data

  • No Model Training: Your uploaded documents, scans, and audio recordings are never used to train public artificial intelligence models.
  • Zero Selling of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or document contents to any third parties or advertisers.
  • Encrypted Transmission: All data transmissions between the Inkto Android app and our backend servers are secured using TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • Ephemeral Media Processing: Temporary audio and PDF compilation artifacts uploaded to Cloudflare R2 storage are retained strictly for the duration of processing and deleted automatically.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure Providers

We work exclusively with enterprise cloud infrastructure providers bound by strict confidentiality and data protection standards:

  • Supabase: For identity authentication, user session persistence, and metadata storage.
  • Cloudflare (Workers & R2 Storage): For low-latency edge computing, SSL termination, and temporary media staging.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API): For multimodal handwriting transcription and speech-to-text processing.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You maintain full ownership of your data. You may delete any document, transcript, or audio draft directly from your Inkto workspace at any time. When deleted locally, associated cloud storage objects are purged immediately.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated metadata, you may submit a request from inside the app or email our support desk.
